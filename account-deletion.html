Account and privacy

Delete your RallyRank account

You can request account deletion in the app. Review what changes before you confirm.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to RallyRank.
  2. Open the Account deletion option in the app.
  3. Review the consequences and confirm your deletion request.

If you are the sole owner of a club, transfer ownership to another active member before deleting your account.

What happens after deletion

Account and club access

Your authentication account and identifying profile information are removed. Your club access is revoked.

Club history

Historical match scores and rankings remain for club records under anonymous player aliases.

Profile photos

Profile photo references are removed. We attempt to delete stored photos right away, but cleanup may continue in the background.

Temporary links

Previously issued temporary photo links or cached copies may remain accessible until they expire.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or complete deletion there, you can request deletion by email without reinstalling it. Our support team will verify that you own the account before processing your request.

Request Account Deletion

Contact rallyrank.dev@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive credentials. Sending an email does not automatically delete your account.
