RallyRank Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

RallyRank is a private amateur badminton club application for recording matches, managing club memberships, maintaining rankings, and viewing player statistics and performance information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RallyRank collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices available to users regarding their information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RallyRank, we may process the following information:

  • Email address used for account registration, authentication, verification, and account recovery.
  • Authentication information necessary to securely operate your RallyRank account.
  • Google account identity information when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Player display name and optional profile information such as a biography.
  • Optional player profile photograph.
  • Club memberships, roles, membership status, invitations, and join requests.
  • Badminton match information including players, guest player names, scores, results, and match history.
  • Ranking, season, statistical, award, and other badminton performance information derived from match records.

RallyRank does not require users to provide location, contacts, microphone recordings, SMS messages, call logs, or similar device information for its normal functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate RallyRank, including to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and recover user accounts.
  • Verify email addresses and process password recovery requests.
  • Create and manage badminton clubs and memberships.
  • Record and display match history.
  • Calculate rankings, player statistics, seasons, Best Partner results, Face-Off results, awards, and related badminton information.
  • Display player profiles and profile photographs to authorized club members where applicable.
  • Support account and club administration.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and integrity of the service.

3. Authentication

RallyRank supports email-based authentication and Google Sign-In.

Email addresses may be used for account verification and password recovery. RallyRank uses authentication services provided through Supabase.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google processes the authentication request and provides the information necessary to authenticate your RallyRank account. RallyRank does not receive your Google password.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

RallyRank uses Supabase for backend services including authentication, PostgreSQL database services, file storage, and server-side functions.

Profile photographs uploaded to RallyRank are stored using RallyRank's backend storage services. Access to profile photographs is restricted according to RallyRank's application authorization rules.

RallyRank may also use an email delivery provider to send account verification and password-recovery messages.

Google services are involved when a user voluntarily chooses Google Sign-In.

5. Sharing of Information

RallyRank does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate RallyRank, such as backend hosting, authentication, storage, email delivery, and Google authentication services.

Club-related information such as player names, match results, rankings, statistics, profile information, and profile photographs may be visible to authorized members of the relevant RallyRank club as required by the application's functionality.

6. Advertising and Tracking

RallyRank does not currently contain third-party advertising and does not use user information for advertising purposes.

7. Profile Photos

Adding a profile photograph is optional.

When a user selects a photograph, RallyRank allows the image to be adjusted before upload. The resulting profile image is stored by RallyRank's backend storage service and is used as part of the player's RallyRank profile.

8. Match and Historical Sporting Data

RallyRank maintains match scores, results, rankings, seasons, statistics, and related sporting records so that clubs can maintain accurate historical records.

Some sporting records may therefore remain after an account is deleted where necessary to preserve the integrity of other players' match histories, rankings, and club records.

When this occurs, the deleted player's personal account identity is removed or anonymized according to RallyRank's account-deletion process. Historical sporting records are not intended to continue identifying the deleted account holder.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request permanent deletion of their RallyRank account.

Account deletion removes the user's authentication account and associated personal account information according to RallyRank's deletion process. Associated profile photographs are also scheduled for removal.

Certain historical badminton records may be retained in anonymized form when required to preserve match, ranking, season, and club history involving other players.

For account-deletion instructions, visit: RallyRank Account Deletion.

10. Data Retention

RallyRank retains account and club information while it is required to provide the service or until it is deleted according to RallyRank's account and club management processes.

Historical sporting records may be retained after account deletion in anonymized form where necessary to preserve legitimate club and match history.

11. Security

RallyRank uses authentication, database authorization rules, access controls, and encrypted network connections to protect information.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but RallyRank is designed to restrict access to club and account information according to authenticated user permissions.

12. Children's Privacy

RallyRank is not specifically designed as a service directed to young children. Clubs and users are responsible for using RallyRank in accordance with applicable laws and the eligibility requirements presented through the application and distribution platform.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RallyRank's functionality, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or account-related enquiries, contact: rallyrank.dev@gmail.com
